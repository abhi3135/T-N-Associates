About T N Associates

A law firm built on decades of trusted practice.

T N Associates was established in 2000 in Kannur, Kerala, by Senior Advocate and Notary T N Divakaran. Over the past two and a half decades, the firm has grown into a recognised legal practice in the region, providing counsel and representation to more than 500 clients across a wide range of civil, criminal, consumer and family matters.

The firm operates from Safiya Complex on Yogasala Road in Kannur, and its team combines long-standing courtroom experience with the ability to handle sensitive client circumstances with discretion, patience and precision. Every matter — whether contested litigation or advisory engagement — receives serious and personalised attention.

The firm’s strength lies in the depth of its senior advocacy and the collaborative structure of its team: experienced advocates who have built reputations in their practice areas alongside junior counsel who bring energy and rigour to day-to-day legal work.

T N Associates is committed to clear communication, responsible legal strategy and the straightforward pursuit of its clients’ legitimate interests in proceedings before Kerala’s courts.

Practice areas

Four core areas. One steadfast practice.

The firm’s work spans civil disputes, criminal proceedings, consumer rights enforcement and sensitive family law matters.

01

Civil Litigation

T N Associates represents clients in a broad range of civil disputes before the courts of Kerala. The firm’s civil practice covers matters concerning immovable property, possession and title disputes, injunctions and interim reliefs, money recovery claims, contractual breaches, tortious liability and related proceedings before civil courts, revenue tribunals and subordinate appellate forums.

The firm places strong emphasis on pre-litigation analysis, structuring the strongest possible claim or defence from the outset, and deploying procedural knowledge to protect clients’ rights at every stage of the proceedings.

Property disputesInjunction & interim reliefsRecovery suitsContractual disputesAppeals
02

Criminal Law

The firm handles a significant volume of criminal work, appearing for the accused at the trial and appellate stages as well as representing complainants pursuing private complaints or supporting prosecution-side proceedings. This covers offences under the Indian Penal Code and under special legislation, including anticipatory bail and regular bail applications, quashing petitions, trial representation and appeal work before the Sessions Court and the High Court of Kerala.

The firm’s approach to criminal matters is grounded in careful examination of the evidence, procedural rights and the proper conduct of a fair trial, ensuring that every accused client receives the full benefit of their legal entitlements.

Bail & anticipatory bailTrial representationPrivate complaintsAppeals & revisionsQuashing petitions
03

Consumer Law

As consumer rights awareness grows in Kerala, the firm provides focused assistance to individuals and businesses in consumer disputes before the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission and the State Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission. The firm handles complaints involving deficiency in service, defective goods, unfair trade practices, medical negligence claims before consumer forums and insurance-related disputes.

Clients receive pragmatic guidance on the most appropriate forum and strategy for their specific complaint, with detailed drafting support and consistent representation throughout the process.

Service deficiencyDefective goodsInsurance disputesUnfair trade practicesConsumer forum appeals
04

Family Law

Family law matters demand a unique combination of legal clarity and human sensitivity. T N Associates handles all forms of matrimonial litigation, including divorce proceedings under personal laws, restitution of conjugal rights, judicial separation, maintenance and alimony claims, custody and guardianship disputes, domestic violence cases and adoption proceedings before the Family Court at Kannur and allied forums.

The firm is acutely aware of the personal stakes involved in family proceedings and conducts this work with particular discretion, measured communication and a genuine effort to arrive at outcomes that protect clients’ long-term interests.

Divorce proceedingsMaintenance & alimonyChild custodyDomestic violenceGuardianship
