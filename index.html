About T N Associates

A law firm built on decades of trusted practice.

T N Associates was established in 2000 in Kannur, Kerala, by Senior Advocate and Notary T N Divakaran. Over the past two and a half decades, the firm has grown into a recognised legal practice in the region, providing counsel and representation to more than 5,000 clients across civil, criminal, consumer and family matters.

The firm operates from Safiya Complex on Yogasala Road in Kannur, and its team combines long-standing courtroom experience with the ability to handle sensitive client circumstances with discretion, patience and precision.

T N Associates is committed to clear communication, responsible legal strategy and the straightforward pursuit of its clients’ legitimate interests in proceedings before Kerala’s courts.

Practice areas

Four core areas. One steadfast practice.

The firm’s work spans civil disputes, criminal proceedings, consumer rights enforcement and sensitive family law matters across Kannur and Kerala.

Civil Litigation

T N Associates represents clients in civil disputes involving property, possession and title disputes, injunctions, money recovery claims, contractual breaches and related proceedings before civil courts, revenue tribunals and appellate forums across Kerala.

The firm places strong emphasis on pre-litigation analysis and procedural knowledge to protect clients’ rights at every stage of proceedings.

Family Law

T N Associates handles matrimonial litigation including divorce, maintenance, custody and guardianship disputes, domestic violence cases and adoption proceedings before the Family Court at Kannur and allied forums.

Family matters are handled with particular discretion and genuine care for clients’ long-term interests and those of children involved.

05

Legal Opinions for Banks & NBFCs

T N Associates provides formal legal opinions to banks, non-banking financial companies and other lending institutions on matters concerning title verification, mortgage validity, security interest creation and the legal standing of proposed transactions. These opinions form a critical part of the due diligence process for credit decisions and are prepared with precision and institutional accountability.

The firm has experience working with scheduled banks and registered NBFCs operating in the Kannur region, providing reliable and clearly reasoned legal documentation for their operational and regulatory requirements.

Title verificationMortgage opinionsBank legal opinionsNBFC complianceDue diligence
06

Notary Services

T N Divakaran, the firm’s founding advocate, is empanelled as a Notary Public under the Government of India. The firm accordingly provides a full range of notarial services including attestation of documents, authentication of signatures, preparation and registration of notarial acts, affidavits, power of attorney verification and certification of copies for use in domestic and international proceedings.

Notary services are available during regular office hours and are widely used by individuals, institutions and businesses requiring officially authenticated documentation for legal, governmental or overseas purposes.

Document attestationAffidavitsPower of attorneySignature authenticationCopy certification
07

Motor Accident Claims (MACT)

T N Associates represents claimants and respondents before the Motor Accidents Claims Tribunal (MACT) in proceedings arising from road accidents, vehicular collisions and related personal injury or fatal accident claims. The firm also represents insurance companies in MACT proceedings, providing sound and procedurally rigorous defence on their behalf.

The firm also represents insurance companies and vehicle owners where instructed, providing balanced and procedurally sound representation on both sides of MACT proceedings.

Claims petitionsCompensation assessmentFatal accident claimsPersonal injuryInsurance disputesLoss of dependency
